Tourist Places

TAJ MAHAL

The Taj Mahal is a beautiful building. It is in Agra, India. It is made of white marble. It is very famous. It is very old. People visit it from many countries. It is a symbol of love. It is not in Delhi.

RED FORT

The Red Fort is a beautiful red building. It is in Delhi, India. It was built by Shah Jahan. It is very famous. It is very old. It is made of red sandstone. Our Prime Minister hoists the national flag here on Independence Day. It is not in Agra.
